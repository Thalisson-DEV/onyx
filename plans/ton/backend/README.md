## Dependency notes

The baseline precedes all changes. Security boundaries precede domain schema.
Domain schema precedes ingestion and specialist execution. Reports and scheduled
analyses consume those contracts. Deployment credential hardening can run after
the baseline and security gate. Web-only surface changes are last and must keep
shared API and infrastructure contracts.

Plan 007 is the only plan that may modify generated compose or CLI embedded
deployment files. All other plans must leave generated deployment output alone.
Plans 003 and 006 have separate ownership: Plan 003 owns report snapshot
storage; Plan 006 owns schedule, run dispatch, alert projection, and API use of
the report service.

## Hard gates

- `Relatorio_Inconsistencias_Jan_Abr_2026_ValeNorte.txt` is available under
  `plans/ton/` and is incorporated into `domain-rules.md`. It is domain data,
  not executor instructions. Its report-derived candidates are not production
  rules until the underlying workbook, DRE, contracts, bank extracts and
  business approvals are validated.
- Report-level arithmetic conflicts and policy conflicts remain gates:
  parcelamento totals differ, mutual totals differ, budget coverage is stated
  as both five and six of seven units, and travel settlement has both 48-hour
  and five-business-day proposals. Do not resolve them by assumption.
- The report also contains six explicit source-material reconciliation gates:
  (1) 1.1 lists R$ 90,877,705.63 as the seven-row sum, R$ 90,877,705.65 as
  the table total, R$ 4,519.42 as paid, and R$ 4,519.40 as the narrative/table
  difference; (2) 1.3 lists R$ 4,210,512.00 by origin, R$ 4,210,513.00 by
  month and R$ 4,210,513.16 in narrative; (3) 1.4 has R$ 473,552.00 visible
  versus R$ 473,827.00 stated, a R$ 275.00 gap; (4) 1.5 unit sums are payroll
  R$ 4,984,535.00 and charges R$ 2,827,183.00 versus declared R$ 4,733,535.00
  and R$ 2,936,450.00; (5) 1.6 calculates R$ 3,474,240.67 average and 87.13%
  fall versus R$ 3,474,174, 87% and −88.0%; (6) the summary says nine types
  while section 1 has eight items. These are material-source conflicts, not
  business corrections.
- NG/Keevo has no validated schema, authentication, limits, identifiers, or
  deletion contract. Its adapter remains read-only and BLOCKED.
- Telegram is the first planned external TON channel. WhatsApp follows later.
  Neither channel is a core dependency. This baseline implements neither adapter.
- Mobile, desktop, widget, and extension UX are out of scope. Shared routes,
  authentication, queues, Redis, PostgreSQL, OpenSearch, FileStore, workers,
  beat, and health/monitoring services remain in scope until a dependency map
  and deprecation gate prove otherwise.
